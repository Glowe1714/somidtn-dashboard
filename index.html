                        Why it matters for Southern Middle Tennessee

                        • Long-term Treasury yields and the 30-year mortgage rate set the cost of financing industrial buildings, spec buildings and housing.

                        • Corporate bond spreads show how easily companies can borrow. Wider spreads usually mean tighter credit for expansion projects.

                        • The dollar's value affects what local manufacturers earn on exports and what foreign companies pay to invest in Tennessee.

                        • Oil, natural gas and copper prices feed into manufacturers' input and freight costs.

                        • Interest rates and growth abroad shape expansion decisions by foreign-owned companies with Tennessee plants.

                                                  Use this dashboard with Claude

                                                  Claude (or another AI assistant) can read this dashboard's data and turn it into briefs, spreadsheets, slides and documents. These steps keep its answers tied to the real numbers.

                                                  Two ways to give Claude the data

                                                  1. Attach a download (works everywhere). Use the Word, PDF or PowerPoint buttons on the Strategic plan and County profiles tabs, or the CSV and Excel buttons above any table, then attach the file to your Claude chat and ask your question.
                                                  2. Give Claude the live data link. Paste https://glowe1714.github.io/somidtn-dashboard/data/manifest.json into the chat. It lists every dataset with its source and last update; each one is at …/data/<id>.json (for example data/counties.json, data/projects.json, data/warn.json). Claude can open links only when web access is available in your Claude app; if it says it cannot, use option 1.

                                                  Optional: a saved instruction

                                                  Paste this at the start of a chat, or into a Claude Project's instructions so every chat in that project follows it.

                                                  Copied. Paste it into Claude.Your browser blocked copying. The text is selected: press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac).
